The short version

Your reading stays yours.

Livro does not run advertising, use tracking technology, or automatically send your reading data to the developer. The app has no developer-operated account or cloud service.

Information stored on your device

Livro stores the information needed to provide its reading features locally in the app's private storage. This may include:

  • EPUB books and articles you choose to import;
  • reading position, bookmarks, notes, finished and archived status;
  • reading settings and accessibility preferences; and
  • reading-session statistics, such as words read, reading time, and reading speed.

This information is not uploaded to a Livro server. Your operating-system backup settings may include app data in an iCloud or device backup; those backups are controlled by you and governed by Apple's policies.

Optional camera access

If you enable eye-guided pacing on a supported device, Livro asks for access to the front camera. Face and gaze information is processed on your device to pause or resume reading when you look away.

Livro does not record camera video or photos, store face data, use face data to identify you, or send camera data off your device. You can disable camera access at any time in iOS Settings.

Network requests you initiate

Livro may connect directly from your device to a third-party website when you choose to browse a public book catalog, download a book, or import an article from a URL. That website may receive standard request information such as your IP address, device/browser details, and the requested URL, according to its own privacy policy.

Livro does not use these requests to build a browsing profile and does not send your imported content to the developer.

Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions are offered through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes your payment and account information. Livro receives only the subscription status needed to unlock paid features; the developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

For information about Apple's handling of purchase data, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics and bug reports

Apple's MetricKit framework may provide performance and crash diagnostics to Livro. These diagnostics are kept in the app's protected local storage and are not automatically transmitted to the developer.

If you choose Report a bug, Livro opens your mail composer and prepares a message with a diagnostic file attached. You can review or cancel the email before sending it. The attachment may include:

  • app version, build number, iOS version, and device model;
  • selected app settings;
  • aggregate reading statistics from the previous seven days; and
  • recent technical error messages.

The prepared diagnostic file is designed not to include book or article text, notes, file paths, contact lists, or advertising identifiers. If you send the report, the email address you use, anything you write, and the attachment will be processed by your email provider and received by the developer for support purposes.

No advertising or tracking

Livro does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites, sell personal information, or use data for targeted advertising.

Your choices and data deletion

You can remove imported items and reset reading data from within Livro. You can also remove the app and its locally stored data through iOS. Copies may remain in device backups until those backups expire or are deleted under your Apple account settings.

You control optional permissions, including camera access, in iOS Settings.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Livro's features or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when that happens.
