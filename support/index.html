Quick help

Start with the common fixes.

Tap a question to see the answer.

How do I import a book?
  1. Open Library.
  2. Tap the add button, then choose Import EPUB.
  3. Select one or more .epub files from the Files picker.

Livro supports EPUB books. It cannot open PDFs, Kindle files, or books protected by DRM.

Why does the reader pause on some words?

Those pauses are intentional. Livro adds short beats at sentence endings, paragraph breaks, em dashes, and colons so the text keeps its rhythm and meaning.

To make them shorter or longer, open Settings → Pacing → Pause length.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Settings → Subscription and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure the iPhone is signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase and has an internet connection.

You can also manage your subscription with Apple.

Eye-guided pacing is not working

Eye-guided pacing requires a supported iPhone with a TrueDepth front camera. Open Settings → Eye-guided, allow camera access, and complete calibration while holding the phone as you normally read.

If camera permission was declined, enable it under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

How do I report a bug?

Email voopat@gmail.com. Please describe the steps that caused the problem and include your Livro version, iOS version, and iPhone model.

Where is my reading data stored?

Your imported books, notes, positions, preferences, and reading statistics stay in Livro’s private storage on your iPhone. Livro does not operate an account or cloud service.

Read the Livro Privacy Policy for complete details.

What to include in a support email

A little context usually makes the problem much faster to identify:

  • Livro version and build, shown under Settings → About;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the steps that led to the problem; and
  • what happened versus what you expected.
